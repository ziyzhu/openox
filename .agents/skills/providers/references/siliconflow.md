## Ox account placement

Ox currently presents SiliconFlow only in China. Do not infer Global support from SiliconFlow's international site without adding and verifying an explicit Ox regional integration.

Its curated catalog begins with a documented free model for first-run use. Free models require identity verification and use fixed rate limits; other models on the same account may be billed.
