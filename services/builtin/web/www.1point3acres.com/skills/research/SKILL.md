# Research 1Point3Acres

Treat forum posts as firsthand reports with unknown completeness, not authoritative policy or a representative sample. Preserve the circumstances that determine whether one person's experience applies to the user's question.

## Search discussions

1. Define the decision, jurisdiction, employer or school, role, immigration category, timeline, and other material constraints.
2. Start with `searchThreads` using exact keywords and a narrow `days` window for time-sensitive topics. Try `semantic` search when terminology varies, then expand the date range only if results are thin.
3. Use `listThreads` when the user wants the newest activity from a known forum or topic rather than answers to a precise question.
4. Open promising discussions with `getThread`. Read later pages when replies contain outcomes, corrections, or changed circumstances.
5. Preserve thread identifiers or links and the dates of relevant posts.

## Evaluate discussion evidence

Compare reports on the same axes:

- event date and policy period
- country, state, consulate, service center, school, or office
- employer, role, seniority, degree, and candidate background
- visa or petition category, filing type, and procedural stage
- whether the post reports direct experience, hearsay, prediction, or advice
- whether later replies confirm, contradict, or resolve the original report

Look for recurring outcomes across independently authored threads. Do not convert a few reports into rates, probabilities, guarantees, or universal rules.

The service exposes thread usernames but not author profiles or posting histories. Do not assign an individual author a credibility rating from a username, writing style, forum status, or isolated post. Assess the specificity and corroboration of the report instead.

## Read structured data

- Use `getVisaBulletin` for the latest returned employment-based dates. State `currentMonth`, category, country of chargeability, and whether a value is a final-action or filing date. Do not substitute one chart for the other.
- Use `getH1bRankings` to compare filing volume or average salary by company, city, or job. Filing counts do not establish approval odds, current openings, sponsorship policy, or the experience of a particular employee.
- Treat both actions as structured snapshots from this service, not legal determinations or substitutes for the responsible government source.

## Conclude

- Stop after three or more materially comparable independent reports support a pattern, or when additional results only repeat existing evidence.
- Separate direct reports, recurring patterns, structured data, disagreement, and your own inference.
- Lead with the practical answer, then state who and when it appears to apply to, contrary evidence, uncertainty, and links.
- For immigration, employment, financial, legal, or safety decisions, flag decisive claims for verification with official sources or qualified professionals; do not imply that the forum performed that verification.
